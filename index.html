Ingredients Table

Ingredient Amount Notes
Pasta 12 ounces For the Roasted Corn Salad we recommend cooking the pasta al-dente
Corn 2 cups You may be able to find pre-roasted corn in the store; if so, you can skip the roasting step and just rehead
Olive Oil 1 ounce Any kind will do but extra virgin usually works the best
Flour Tortillas 3 Tortillas Extra tortillas can be useful to have on hand, and you can substitute corn tortillas to make this dish Gluten-Free
Cheese 4 ounces We recommend hand-shredding for best results as pre-shredded blends can have coatings that prevent proper melting
